## Full description
Bout is a daily brain game you play against your mates.

Everyone in your crew gets the same single puzzle each day. Solve it on your own
schedule — one to three minutes — and your result feeds a season-long crew ladder
with weight-class divisions, a finals week, and a fresh reset each season.

The puzzle is the heartbeat. The crew league is the product.

• One bout a day — no grinding, no infinite play.
• A rotating roster: word, logic, number, visual, memory, trivia-lite, and a harder
  weekend feature. Different brains win on different days.
• A live ladder that reads like a clean results sheet.
• Streaks, an improvement bonus, and a daily pick'em side-bet.
• Weight-class divisions with promotion and relegation between seasons.
• No crew yet? Get matched into a house league of similar players and start today.
• An optional, opt-in live race for the adrenaline crowd.

No real-money stakes. No public feed, no followers, no algorithm. Just a closed
competition among people who know each other. Stakes are pride, streaks, and bragging rights.
