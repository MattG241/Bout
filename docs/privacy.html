Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 June 2026

Bout ("Bout", "we", "us") operates the Bout mobile app — a daily brain game played within private crews and matched "house" leagues. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Bout you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account data — a handle you choose, an account identifier, and, if you sign up with email, your email address.
  • Gameplay data — your puzzle attempts, scores, accuracy, solve times, streaks, predictions, and league standings.
  • Device data — a push-notification token and your device timezone, used to deliver the daily notification in your local morning.
  • Purchase data — subscription and entitlement status from RevenueCat and the app stores. We never receive or store your payment-card details; Apple and Google process all payments.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, or photos, and we do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. There is no public feed, no followers, and no algorithmic content discovery.

How we use your information

To run the daily game and your crew league: deliver the day's puzzle, validate and score attempts on our servers, maintain streaks and standings, send the daily notification, provide premium features to subscribers, and keep the competition fair and free of cheating.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (running the game), on the basis of your consent (notifications), and for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service.

How we share information

Your handle, scores, and standings are visible to members of leagues you belong to. We do not sell your data. We use the following processors strictly to operate the app: Supabase (backend, database, authentication), Expo (push notification delivery), and RevenueCat (subscription management).

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, which permanently and irreversibly erases your profile, attempts, streaks, predictions, and standings.

Security

Competitive data is server-authoritative and protected with row-level security. Puzzle solutions are never sent to your device before you submit, and submissions are validated, timed, and scored on our servers.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can delete everything in the app, or contact us via our support page.

Children

Bout is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own by us or our processors, with appropriate safeguards in place.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we'll revise the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
