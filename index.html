How it works

Simple enough to keep up with.

01

One puzzle, every day

Everyone gets the same bout. A different type each day of the week, so it never settles into a routine you can autopilot.

02

Scored on how well, not how fast

Accuracy carries the score. Speed is worth a little, never enough to turn it into a reflex contest.

03

Your crew, your ladder

Start a gym, share the code, and settle it over a season — with weight classes so you are matched against people at your level.

04

Keep the streak

Play every day and your multiplier climbs. Miss one and you start again, which is rather the point.

Fair play

Never pay-to-win.

The daily bout and the ladder are free for everyone, forever. The Season Pass adds depth — deeper stats, unlimited practice puzzles, challenges you set for your crew — and it is built so that none of it can move your position on the ladder. Not as a promise, as a design constraint.
