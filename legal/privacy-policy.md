# Bout — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-17_

This is a starting-point policy for the Bout app. Have it reviewed by a lawyer and host it at
`https://bout.app/privacy` before submitting to the App Store / Google Play (both stores require
a reachable privacy policy URL).

## Who we are
Bout ("we", "us") operates the Bout mobile app, a daily brain game played within private crews
and matched house leagues.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** a handle you choose, an account identifier, and (if you sign up with email)
  your email address.
- **Gameplay data:** your puzzle attempts, scores, streaks, predictions, and league standings.
- **Device data:** a push notification token and your device timezone (to deliver the daily
  notification in your local morning).
- **Purchases:** subscription/entitlement status via RevenueCat and the app stores. We do **not**
  receive or store your payment card details — payments are handled by Apple and Google.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and we do **not**
use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## How we use it
To run the daily game and your crew league: deliver puzzles, score attempts server-side, maintain
streaks and standings, send the daily notification, and provide premium features to subscribers.

## Sharing
Your handle, scores, and standings are visible to members of leagues you belong to. We do not sell
your data or share it for advertising. We use Supabase (backend/hosting), Expo (push delivery), and
RevenueCat (subscriptions) as processors.

## Your choices and rights
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete account permanently erases your profile, attempts,
  streaks, predictions, and standings. This is irreversible.
- **Notifications:** turn the daily drop on/off in Settings or your OS settings.
- Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR/CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, or export your
  data — contact us at the address below.

## Children
Bout is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13.

## Data retention
We keep your data while your account is active. Deleting your account removes it promptly.
