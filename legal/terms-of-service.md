## The service
Bout is a daily brain game played within private crews and matched house leagues. One puzzle is
released per day. Scores feed a season-long ladder with weight-class divisions and a finals week.

## Fair play
- Puzzles, answer validation, timing, and scoring are server-authoritative. Attempting to cheat,
  automate, reverse-engineer, or extract puzzle solutions is prohibited and may result in removal.
- One attempt per puzzle per day. Duplicate or tampered submissions are rejected.

## No wagering
Bout contains **no real-money stakes, wagering, or gambling of any kind.** Stakes are pride,
streaks, and bragging rights only.
